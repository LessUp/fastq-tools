## How to use Academy

Academy is the learning layer. It points you to workflow-oriented entry points without replacing the detailed guides that live under Reference.

- Start with [Workflows](../workflows/) to choose an adoption path.
- Move to [Reference](../reference/) when you need the concrete guide, API, or developer manual for a task.
